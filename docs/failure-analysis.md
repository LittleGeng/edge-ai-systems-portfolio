# Failure analysis and stop decisions

**Resolved evidence gap.** Three original August 4 VLA-01 audits are now archived locally and agree on 432 episodes, 52,970 frames and all 377 parquet hashes. The August 8-9 activity was synchronization and `verify-only` integrity verification, not new experiment execution.

1. **SmolVLA misses the target by construction.** Measured steady throughput is 0.837 Hz versus 30 Hz. A queue can hide jitter but cannot create fresh actions; the 10 s replay produced 8/300 fresh ticks and 284 safe-fallback ticks.
2. **Energy is not defensible.** Tegrastats exposed component rails but no confirmed total-module rail. `energy_j_per_action` remains null instead of summing overlapping rails.
3. **VLA task success is modest.** Main-condition success is 13.3%; this is a feasibility result, not production readiness.
4. **Scope is deliberately narrow.** Orin performance uses synthetic inputs; no real sensor, actuator, network, or safety-loop latency is included.
5. **Compile passes a point-estimate screen, not statistical non-inferiority.** Paired LIBERO success was 20.0% eager versus 22.0% compile, delta +2.0%, bootstrap 95% CI [-10.0%, +14.0%]. The point estimate is inside the +/-5 percentage-point rule, but the interval is not; the Orin candidate also raised RAM by 1099 MB, showed dynamic KV-cache recompilation warnings, and missed the 30 Hz deadline.
6. **EdgeDiffusion step reduction fails the quality gate.** In paired Push-T windows, only 100 steps passed; 20/10/8/6 steps reduced controller P50 to 217.6/88.4/80.4/65.2 ms but all had 0% high-quality completion and were rejected. On Orin, 6 steps meets the 50 ms timing gate while the 100-step quality reference misses it; no measured count passes both gates.
7. **SmolVLA async scheduling fails the injected-latency task gate.** On physical Orin, increasing refill threshold from 0.5 to 0.8 raises fresh-action ticks from 68.7% to 86.7% but stale ticks from 100 to 201. In the LIBERO latency-envelope test, async success fell from 24% to 8% and from 40% to 4%; both exceed the +/-5 percentage-point limit.
8. **SmolVLA visual downsampling fails the paired task gate.** Directly changing the frozen policy input from 512x512 to 256x256 reduced paired LIBERO success from 20.0% to 0.0%, a -20.0% delta with CI [-32.0%, -10.0%]. The candidate is rejected; any lower-resolution deployment requires retraining or distillation and the same quality gate.
9. **A short 384px fine-tune does not recover the task-quality loss.** The 6000-step rank-32 seed-43 recovery candidate reached 10.0% versus 20.0% for paired eager 512x512, missing the predeclared 15.0% minimum. The planned three-seed and 20k-step expansion was stopped rather than spending more GPU hours on a failed gate.
10. **Targeted INT8 reduces weight storage but not end-to-end action latency on this Orin path.** Weight-only and dynamic INT8 both passed the action-difference gate, yet first-action P50 changed from 1202.12 ms to 1415.67/5450.43 ms. Both are rejected; the result does not generalize beyond this TorchAO version, targeted layer set, model, input and Jetson runtime.

The next optimization gate is to keep 100 steps as the quality reference and only test a lower-step or distilled policy after it has an explicit paired quality budget. SmolVLA stage profiling already identifies denoise VLM forward (60.60%) and prefix embedding (27.04%) as the dominant measured paths; future work should move to kernel/export-level analysis before TensorRT/ONNX or quantization-aware retraining.
